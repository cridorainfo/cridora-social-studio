# Cridora Social Studio

Makes Instagram, Facebook and X posts in the Cridora look, in English and Arabic, with gold and silver rates and a daily caption.

Open the page, check the rates against cridora.com, pick a design and download the PNG.

Rates are saved here about every 30 minutes by a scheduled job (`refresh-rates.mjs`): the public Cridora rates, and a silver retail
reading from Khaleej Times (Al Kananah, Dubai). The page shows the time of the rates and the date of the silver reading. Edit any
number under "Rates and numbers" before posting.
